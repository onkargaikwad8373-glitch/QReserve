# 🍽️ QReserve – Smart Restaurant Queue Management System

**QReserve** is a web-based restaurant queue management system designed to streamline customer registration and waiting queue operations through QR-based registration and digital tokens.

Customers can scan a restaurant-specific QR code, register themselves, and receive a digital queue token. Restaurant staff can efficiently monitor and manage waiting customers through a centralized dashboard.

## 📌 Problem Statement

During peak hours, restaurants often face challenges in managing customer waiting queues. Manual registration and queue management can result in overcrowding, disorganized queues, communication gaps, and increased workload for restaurant staff.

## 💡 Our Solution

QReserve provides a digital platform for efficient restaurant queue management.

Customers can scan a restaurant-specific QR code, complete their registration, and receive a digital queue token. Restaurant staff can manage waiting customers, update queue statuses, and monitor queue activity through a centralized dashboard.

## ✨ Key Features

### 👥 Customer Features

- QR-based self-registration.
- No application installation required.
- Automatic digital token generation.
- Queue position tracking.
- Queue status visibility.

### 🧑‍🍳 Restaurant Staff Features

- Centralized management dashboard.
- View registered customers.
- Manage waiting queues.
- Update customer queue statuses.
- Monitor queue positions.

## 👤 User Roles

| Role | Responsibility |
